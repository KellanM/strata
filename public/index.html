How to play

A shape is hidden inside the block. Chisel away every cube that isn't part of it.

A number tells you how many cubes in that row stay. 3 means they're split into two groups, 4 into three or more.

Tap to chisel. Hold (or right-click) to mark a cube you know stays, which protects it.

Drag to turn the block and pinch to zoom. Drag a diamond handle along an edge to peel layers away and see inside. Double-tap empty space to reset the view.

Stars: one for uncovering the shape, two for only a few slips, three for none. Marking and peeling never cost a star, and there's no rush.

Best on your Home Screen: full screen, with sound, and it plays offline.

iPhone: Safari's then Add to Home Screen.
Android: Chrome's ⋮ then Add to Home screen (or Install app); Samsung Internet's ≡ then Add page to → Home screen.
