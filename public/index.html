How to play

Every stone hides an object. Chisel away every cube that isn't part of it.

3
3 of the object's cubes are in this row, running across, down or back
3
they're split into two groups
4
three groups or more
✕
a slip: you chiselled a cube that belongs. It's kept for you
◆◆◆
for no slips, two for a few, one for finishing. No rush

Stuck? Wait a moment and a row with a move will glow. A slip can be taken back with ↺, so a stone can still be finished clean.
