Strata · Privacy

Unsupervised Rock · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Strata is a quiet puzzle game, and it keeps to itself. We do not collect any information about you.

What Strata keeps

Your progress (the stones you have uncovered, your stars and your settings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What Strata does not do

Strata has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use your advertising identifier, your location, your contacts or your photos. It contains no third-party code that collects data. It plays fully offline.

Purchases

This version of Strata has no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

Because your progress lives only on your device, deleting Strata from your device deletes it too. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or return.

Children

Strata is made for a general audience and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version of Strata changes any of this, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mythen.io.
