What Strata keeps

Your progress (the stones you have uncovered, your gems, your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run accounts, and we do not send your progress anywhere.

Ads

Strata is free and shows a small number of ads, provided by AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it works with. To show and measure ads, these services may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone and iPad you will be asked whether apps may track you; Strata works the same whichever you choose. In the EU, UK and similar regions you will be asked for consent first. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings.

AppLovin's privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy. [The list of mediated networks will be added here.]

Remove Ads

If you buy Remove Ads, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive only confirmation that it was bought, not your payment details.

Children

Strata is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
