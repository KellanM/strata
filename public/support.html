Unsupervised Rock

Strata is a 3D nonogram: a block of stone with an object hidden inside, and numbers on its faces that tell you where it is. Peel away everything that isn't the object, and it is yours.

How do I play?

On the title, tap the glowing stone. The first time, twelve short lessons follow and teach everything as you go: how to turn the stone, how to read the numbers, how to chisel a cube away and how to mark one you mean to keep. After that, the map leads from stone to stone. You can return to the lessons at any time from the first stone on the map, and the rules are always a tap away, in Settings on the map or in the pause menu during a puzzle.

What do the numbers mean?

Each number says how many cubes in its row belong to the hidden object. A plain number means those cubes sit together in one run; a circled number means they are split into two groups; a number in a square means three or more groups. A 0 means the whole row can go.

Is there a time limit?

No. A small clock counts up, but nothing runs out, and time never costs you a star. Stars only reward a steady hand.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere, so Strata plays the same with or without a connection. Progress is not shared between devices.

How do I start over?

Deleting Strata from your device erases its progress. Install it again and you will begin at the first stone. There is no copy anywhere else, so this cannot be undone.
