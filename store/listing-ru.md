## Subtitle (30)

**Тихий японский кроссворд в 3D**

(29 / 30 characters)

The English subtitle is "A calm 3D puzzle game". In Russian the genre has a household name, японский кроссворд, which is what players search for, so it takes the place of "puzzle game"; головоломки, in the name, covers that search. Тихий ("quiet") is the description's own word for the game.

## Keywords (100)

```
нонограмма,логика,пазл,кубики,антистресс,мозг,числа
```

(51 characters; 96 / 100 bytes)

Comma-separated with no spaces. Leaves out words already in the name or subtitle (Strata, каменные, головоломки, японский, кроссворд, 3D, тихий), which Apple indexes anyway. Picross is a Nintendo trademark and is not used. нонограмма is the puzzle's other Russian name; пазл and головоломка are the two words Russian players use for "puzzle"; антистресс is the Russian store's word for a relaxing game, standing in for "relaxing" and "zen"; мозг for "brain"; кубики for "cube" and "voxel". The English "offline" and "chisel" didn't fit the 100 bytes: "без интернета" is in the description instead.

## Notes for the reviewer

- **Stars → самоцветы.** The English description says "stars"; the game itself calls them gems (◆), so this uses самоцветы, the game's word, as the Japanese and Chinese pages do.
- **"A bonus stone"** is written святилище — бонусный камень, so the store word matches the map's святилище.
- **Chapters → миры.** The English description says "chapters" and "lands"; the game says worlds (мир), so this does too.
- **Chapter names** in the description are the in-game ones, declined (Первый свет → от Первого света, Сад мхов, Праздник фонарей, Глубина, Звездопад).
