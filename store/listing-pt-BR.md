## App name (30)

**Strata: Quebra-Cabeça de Pedra**

(30 / 30 characters)

"Quebra-cabeça" is the everyday Brazilian word for a puzzle game, and the one people search. If 30 on the nose is a worry, **Strata: Enigmas de Pedra** (24) says the same with a weaker search word. The home-screen label stays **Strata**.

## Subtitle (30)

**Jogo de lógica 3D relaxante**

(27 / 30 characters)

"Jogo de lógica" is how Brazilian stores and blogs file nonograms, sudoku and the like; "relaxante" stands for the English "calm" and is the word the genre's players search with ("jogos relaxantes").

## Keywords (100)

```
nonograma,pintar,números,enigma,cubos,voxel,zen,calmo,offline,cérebro,raciocínio,hanjie,griddlers
```

(97 / 100 characters; 100 bytes, as three letters are accented)

Comma-separated with no spaces. Leaves out words already in the name or subtitle (strata, quebra-cabeça, pedra, jogo, lógica, 3D, relaxante), which Apple indexes anyway. Picross is a Nintendo trademark and is not used.
- **nonograma** is the Brazilian name for the genre; **pintar** and **números** catch "pintar por números" and "jogo de números", the phrases casual players use for picture-logic puzzles.
- **cérebro** and **raciocínio** stand in for the English "brain" ("jogos para o cérebro", "jogos de raciocínio" are common Brazilian searches); **enigma** widens past "quebra-cabeça".
- **hanjie** and **griddlers** are kept from the English list for players who know the genre by those names.

## Notes for the reviewer

- **Stars → gemas.** The English description says "stars" ("earn their stars", "never costs you a star"); the game calls them gems (◆), so this uses **gemas**, the game's word, as the Japanese and Chinese pages do.
- **"A bonus stone waits in every chapter"** is written as the map's **santuário**, so the store word matches the game.
- **"Chapters" → mundos.** The game's map and Finds say "world" (mundo); the store says the same, so "fifteen lands" and "15 chapters" both became **mundos**.
- **Chapter names** in the description are the in-game ones (Primeira Luz, Jardim de Musgo, Festa das Lanternas, As Profundezas, Chuva de Estrelas).
- **"Sem tempo-limite"** for "No timers": the game shows a small clock that counts up, so "sem cronômetro" (no stopwatch) would be untrue. What the English means is that nothing runs out.
